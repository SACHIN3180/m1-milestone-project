My 2nd official 10K

By Palash Biswas · Kolkata, West Bengal, India · MarathonMitra

AuthorPalash Biswas
Date11 October 2026
LocationKolkata, West Bengal
Categorystory
EventDOUR – A Mahalaya Marathon | Edition 2


I am absolutely thrilled to share that I've officially hit a new Personal Best (PB) today! 🏅 The feeling of breaking my own record is indescribable. All those early morning training sessions, the discipline, and the quiet hard work have finally culminated in this single, triumphant moment. I am feeling incredibly happy, overwhelmed with gratitude, and so proud of what my body can achieve. ✨

What a perfect way to kick off the festive season! Here’s to setting new goals, trusting the process, and chasing them down. 🙌❤️
